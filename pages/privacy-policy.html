1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

FD System Holdings LLC respects your privacy. Our website (fdsystemholdings.com) is designed as an informational static web property. We do not actively collect, harvest, store, sell, or monetize any personally identifiable information (PII) from visitors to our website.

While we do not operate databases to track visitors, standard internet infrastructure and web browsers automatically process basic technical and operational metadata to deliver the site securely and reliably.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

When you visit fdsystemholdings.com, FD System Holdings LLC does not:

  • Require user registration, accounts, or logins.
  • Deploy behavioral tracking pixels, third-party advertising cookies, or cross-site analytics trackers.
  • Collect personal identifiers such as your name, physical address, phone number, or financial details through the website.
  • Sell, rent, or trade visitor data to data brokers or third parties.

3. Basic Web Information Automatically Processed

To render web pages, maintain network security, and ensure site performance, basic technical information is automatically handled in two ways:

A. Infrastructure & Hosting Logs

Our static web hosting and DNS infrastructure providers (such as GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) automatically record standard server access logs when your browser requests a page. This basic technical data includes:

  • IP Address: Temporarily logged to route traffic and mitigate DDoS attacks or malicious activity.
  • HTTP Request Metadata: Browser type and version (User-Agent string), operating system, referring URL, requested page path, and the date/time stamp of the request.

Note: FD System Holdings LLC does not download, aggregate, or link these infrastructure logs to identify individual users.

B. Local Browser Storage & Caching

Your web browser may automatically save basic site assets locally on your device—such as HTML, CSS, fonts, images, or basic display preferences (e.g., light/dark mode states)—via standard HTTP caching or localStorage. This information remains strictly on your device to speed up page load times and is never transmitted back to FD System Holdings LLC.

4. Direct Communications

If you choose to contact us directly via an email link provided on the website (such as web@fdsystemholdings.com), we will receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and conduct standard business correspondence.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or repositories (such as GitHub or LinkedIn). FD System Holdings LLC is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our website operations or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised Effective Date.
